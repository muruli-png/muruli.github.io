Circulation Workflow

Document No. LIB/CIR/SOP/01  ·  Version 1.0
Covers member registration through issue, return, renewal, holds, and fines.
Download the Word version (.docx) ↓

Purpose

This procedure defines the workflow for circulation services — member registration, issue (check-out), return (check-in), renewal, reservation/hold, fine management, and stock/inventory-related circulation controls.

Scope

  • Registration and management of all categories of library members (students, research scholars, faculty, staff)
  • Issue, return, renewal, and reservation of circulating items
  • Overdue notices, fines/penalties, and lost/damaged item handling
  • Inter-library loan (ILL) requests routed through the circulation desk

Objectives

  1. Ensure quick, accurate, and courteous issue/return transactions.
  2. Maintain reliable, real-time records of item status and member borrowing history.
  3. Enforce borrowing rules (loan period, quota, fines) fairly and consistently.
  4. Recover overdue material promptly and minimise loss.
  5. Support equitable access through an efficient reservation/hold system.

General workflow

1Member registration

  • Verify identity/enrolment and collect a passport photo, if required.
  • Create a borrower record with a unique library card/ID number, category, and applicable loan quota/period.
  • Issue a library card and record its validity period.

2Issue (check-out)

  • Verify the member's card/ID and confirm no blocking dues, overdues, or debarment.
  • Scan/enter the item barcode; confirm availability.
  • Record the transaction with a due date as per the loan period.

3Return (check-in)

  • Scan/enter the returned item; inspect for damage.
  • Close the loan and update item status to available (or route to the hold-shelf if reserved).
  • Calculate and collect any overdue fine at this point.

4Renewal

  • On request, check that the item has no pending reservation and the renewal limit is not exceeded.
  • Extend the due date and record the renewal count.

5Reservation / hold

  • Accept a hold request on an item currently on loan; queue requests in order received.
  • On return, route the item to the next member in the queue and notify them with a pickup deadline.

6Overdue management

  • Run overdue reports at defined intervals.
  • Send reminder notices at escalating stages; apply fines as per policy.

7Lost / damaged item handling

  • Record a lost/damaged item report from the member or found during return/stock-check.
  • Apply the replacement-cost/processing-fee policy; close the loan once settled.

8Membership renewal / withdrawal

  • Renew membership annually or per institutional cycle; verify continued eligibility.
  • On withdrawal, clear all loans/dues and issue a No-Dues certificate before final withdrawal.

9Stock verification support

  • Flag items charged out during stock-taking so they are not wrongly marked missing.

10Record-keeping and reporting

  • Maintain the borrower register, transaction logs, fine collection register, and lost-item register.
  • Generate periodic circulation statistics for management review.
