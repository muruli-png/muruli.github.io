Standard Operating Procedures for Library Services

A working reference for how core library operations run at the Department of Library and Information Science, Central University of Himachal Pradesh — written for day-to-day use at the desk and for classroom demonstration of the Koha ILS.

Procedures on file
  • SOP 01

    Acquisition

    Selection, verification, ordering, receiving, accessioning, and payment — with a step-by-step mapping to Koha's Acquisitions module.
  • SOP 02

    Serials Control

    Subscription, prediction, check-in, claiming, routing, and binding of journals and periodicals — mapped to Koha's Serials module.
  • SOP 03

    Circulation

    Member registration, issue, return, renewal, holds, and fines — mapped to Koha's Circulation module.
  • SOP 04

    Technical Processing

    Cataloguing, classification, authority control, and physical processing — mapped to Koha's Cataloguing module.
How to use this manual. Each procedure page opens with the general, system-agnostic workflow — usable regardless of which library management system is in place — followed by a table mapping every step to the matching screen in Koha, and a suggested walkthrough for classroom or lab demonstration. A downloadable Word (.docx) version of each SOP is linked at the top of its page.
